Kyzart is a small village in the mountains of Naryn region and the classic starting point for horse treks to Song-Kul lake. There is no direct bus to Kyzart, but getting there from Bishkek is easy. Here are four ways to do it, from the cheapest to the most comfortable.

## 1. Marshrutka (minibus) to Kochkor

Marshrutkas to Kochkor leave from the bus station in Bishkek. This is the cheapest option. Minibuses leave once they are full, so it's best to come in the morning.

Kochkor is the nearest big village to Kyzart. From there, take a local taxi to Kyzart (see option 3 below).

## 2. Shared taxi with the help of your guides

If you're traveling with us, just tell your guides when you want to arrive. They know the local drivers and people from the area who are driving from Bishkek toward Kyzart, and they will ask them to pick you up. This works like a shared taxi: you share the car with other passengers and the price is lower than a private taxi.

## 3. Yandex Taxi

You can order a Yandex Taxi from Bishkek:

- **Directly to Kyzart.** This is the most comfortable option, door to door.
- **To Kochkor.** Usually cheaper. In Kochkor, find a local taxi going to Kyzart village.

## 4. Share a ride with other travelers

Plenty of travelers head to Song-Kul every summer. Look for travel companions in Facebook groups for travelers in Kyrgyzstan or on [NomadTable](https://nomadtable.com), then share a taxi or a rented car to split the cost.
