We arrange border permits for every guest traveling with us into Kyrgyzstan's border zones — including Aksai, the Arpa valley, Kel-Suu, Chatyr-Kol, Enilchek and other destinations. You don't need to navigate the paperwork yourself: we prepare and arrange the permit before your trip.

## Which zones require a permit

A border permit is required to visit areas along Kyrgyzstan’s borders with China, Kazakhstan and Tajikistan. One permit can cover several regions, but every destination you plan to visit needs to be listed in advance.

### Issyk-Kul Region

- Karkara Valley
- Enilchek Town
- Inylchek Glacier
- Ak-Shyirak Village
- Karasay Checkpoint
- Echikilitash Checkpoint
- Sary-Jaz Mountain Range
- Khan-Tengri National Park

### Naryn Region

- Kel-Suu Lake
- Chatyr-Kol Lake
- Aksay Valley
- Arpa Valley
- Torugart Checkpoint

### Chuy Region

- Chon-Kemin National Park
- Ak-Jol Checkpoint

### Osh Region

- Irkeshtam Checkpoint
- Bor-Dobo Checkpoint

### Batken Region

- Leilek Checkpoint
- Kyzyl-Bel Checkpoint

### Talas Region

- Chong-Kapka Checkpoint
- Chaldybar Checkpoint

## Cost and timing

- **Cost:** $25 per person
- **Processing time:** 4 to 7 days after documents are submitted
- **Validity:** 30 days
- **When to apply:** at least 2 weeks before your planned trip into the border zone
- **Format:** an original paper document with stamps and signatures — a digital copy is not accepted at checkpoints

## What we need from you

To start the paperwork, send us:

- a clear photo or scan of your passport's main page
- your travel dates and the planned date of entry into the border zone
- the list of destinations you plan to visit
- a WhatsApp contact for confirming details

For a group, we need each participant’s details — the permit is issued per person.

## How to book

If you're planning a trip to Aksai, Arpa, Kel-Suu or another border zone, message us on WhatsApp with your dates and destination. We'll tell you what documents are needed, confirm the timing, and arrange the border permit so you can focus on the trip.
