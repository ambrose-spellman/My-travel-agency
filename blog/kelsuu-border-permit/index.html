Do You Need a Permit for Kel-Suu? Here's How It Works

← All Guides

Kel-Suu is one of the most striking lakes in Kyrgyzstan, but it sits inside the country's border zone with China — so every visitor needs a permit to enter the area, not just foreign tourists.

The good news is that you don't need to deal with any paperwork yourself. We arrange the permit for every traveler joining one of our Kel-Suu tours.

All we need from you is your passport details, sent to us at least two weeks before your trip. That's enough time for the permit to be processed before you arrive.

If you're planning a Kel-Suu trip, message us on WhatsApp with your travel dates and we'll let you know exactly what we need and by when.
