About Us

Local guides, authentic experiences

Our Story

We are a small, local team based in At-Bashy, Naryn region. We grew up riding these valleys and know the gorges, passes and families along the route to Tash-Rabat, Chatyr-Kol and Kel-Suu firsthand. We started this company to share those routes the way we experience them: at a personal pace, with real local hospitality, not as a stop on a fixed group itinerary.

I spent years living abroad and working in an office, until I started traveling and realized I didn't want to go back. Coming home to Kyrgyzstan, I fell in love with my country all over again and decided to show it to the world. One of my goals is to help tourism grow in my hometown, At-Bashy, so local people can meet travelers and earn a living from it.

Our Values

Local Guides
Our guides are local residents who spend their lives on horseback and behind the wheel in these mountains — herding livestock and working with travelers for more than 10 years. They know the weather, the terrain and how to handle the animals.
Personal Approach
We want every tour to leave a great impression of our country and our company. That means understanding what you want, and doing our best to build the trip around your preferences.
Safety First
First-aid certified team, calm and reliable horses, a safety briefing for beginners, and clear, honest communication throughout your trip.
Local Development
We want tourism to benefit local families directly — meeting new people, offering services, and earning an income from it.
