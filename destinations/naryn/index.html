Naryn

High inner Tien Shan — Tash-Rabat, Song-Kul and the road to Kel-Suu

About Naryn

Naryn region covers the high inner Tien Shan: wide river valleys, jailoo pastures above 3,000 m, and the roads that lead to Song-Kul, Kel-Suu and the old Silk Road post of Tash-Rabat. This is where most of our own tours run — it's the least visited and most dramatic part of Kyrgyzstan.

Best timeJune–September
From Bishkek5–7 hours by car
ActivitiesHorse riding, 4x4, hiking

Highlights

Tash-Rabat Caravanserai
A stone caravanserai from the 15th century, tucked into a quiet side valley that was once a stop on the Silk Road. One of the best-preserved historic buildings in the country.
Song-Kul
A high alpine lake at about 3,000 m, surrounded by open pastures where shepherd families set up yurt camps every summer. One of the best places in Kyrgyzstan to experience nomadic life firsthand.
Kel-Suu
A remote turquoise lake between granite walls near the Chinese border, reached on horseback over the Bogoshtu Pass or by 4x4. One of the most dramatic and least-visited places in the country.
At-Bashy Valley
Our home valley, between the At-Bashy and Jaman-Too ranges — gorges, summer pastures and the starting point for our horse treks toward Tash-Rabat.

Getting There

Naryn town is reached via the Bishkek-Torugart highway, a scenic drive over the Dolon Pass. From there, smaller roads and 4x4 tracks lead further into the mountains.

• Private car transfer from Bishkek (included in tours)
• Shared taxi from Bishkek's western bus station
• 4x4 required beyond Naryn town for most routes
