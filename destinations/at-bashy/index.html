At-Bashy

Our home valley: gorges, pastures and the road to Tash-Rabat

About At-Bashy

At-Bashy is our home: a valley in the Naryn region between the At-Bashy and Jaman-Too ranges, with gorges, summer pastures and the road south to Tash-Rabat and Chatyr-Kol. It is also the gateway to Kel-Suu Lake in the Kok-Kiya valley near the Chinese border.

Best timeMay–September
From Bishkek≈ 6–7 hours by car
ActivitiesHorse riding, 4x4, hiking
DifficultyEasy to Challenging

Highlights

Tash-Rabat
Stone caravanserai in a quiet side valley, once a stop on the Silk Road.
Chatyr-Kol
High plateau lake near the Torugart road, with wide views of the ranges.
Achakaiyndy Gorges
The valley where our horse routes begin, with dramatic rock walls.

Getting There

We pick you up in Bishkek and drive via Kochkor and Naryn to At-Bashy. The drive takes most of a day with stops on the way.

• Private car transfer from Bishkek (included in tours)
• Shared taxi to Naryn from Bishkek's western bus station
• 4x4 for mountain roads beyond At-Bashy
