Kel-Suu

A turquoise lake at 3,500 m between granite walls

About Kel-Suu

Kel-Suu is a turquoise alpine lake sitting at about 3,500 m in the Kok-Kiya valley, close to the Chinese border. It is reached on horseback over the Bogoshtu Pass or by 4x4 via Tash-Rabat and Aksay — one of the most remote and dramatic places in Kyrgyzstan.

Best timeJuly–August
From Bishkek≈ 8–9 hours by car, plus a ride or drive in

Border Zone Permit

Getting There

Kel-Suu is reached either by a multi-day horse trek over the Bogoshtu Pass, or by 4x4 via Tash-Rabat, Arpa and Aksay, arriving at the shore directly.

• Horse trek via Bogoshtu Pass (3–4 days from At-Bashy)
• 4x4 route via Tash-Rabat and Aksay (1–2 days from Bishkek)
• Border permit arranged by us — passport details needed in advance
