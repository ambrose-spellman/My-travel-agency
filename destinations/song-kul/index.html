Song-Kul

Alpine lake and summer yurt camps of shepherd families

About Song-Kul

Song-Kul is a high alpine lake at about 3,000 m surrounded by open pastures. Every summer, shepherd families move up with their herds and set up yurt camps along the shore — it is one of the best places in Kyrgyzstan to experience nomadic life firsthand.

Best timeJune–September
From Bishkek≈ 5–6 hours by car
ActivitiesHorse riding, 4x4
DifficultyEasy to Moderate

Highlights

Yurt Camps
Stay with shepherd families in traditional yurts by the lake.
Horseback Rides
Easy rides around the shore and up into the surrounding pastures.
Sunrise & Sunset
The light over the lake at dawn and dusk is a highlight for photographers.

Getting There

Song-Kul is reached via Kochkor by 4x4 or on horseback, climbing over the Kalmak-Ashuu or Moldo-Ashuu passes onto the plateau.

• Private car/4x4 transfer from Bishkek (included in tours)
• Shared taxi to Kochkor from Bishkek, then 4x4 onward
• Horseback approach available from several trailheads
