Sary-Chelek, Arslanbob & Osh

Forests, walnut groves and the ancient south

About Sary-Chelek, Arslanbob & Osh

The south of Kyrgyzstan is a different country again: the Sary-Chelek Biosphere Reserve's mountain lakes, the world's largest walnut forest at Arslanbob, and the ancient city of Osh with its bazaar and Sulaiman-Too. A green, forested contrast to the high Naryn plateau.

Best timeMay–October
From Bishkek≈ 10–12 hours by car, or a short flight to Osh
ActivitiesHiking, 4x4, culture
DifficultyModerate

Highlights

Sary-Chelek Lakes
A chain of mountain lakes inside a UNESCO biosphere reserve.
Arslanbob Walnut Forest
The largest natural walnut forest in the world, with waterfalls and viewpoints.
Osh Bazaar & Sulaiman-Too
Central Asia's oldest city, its bazaar and the sacred Sulaiman-Too mountain.

Getting There

Most travelers fly from Bishkek to Osh (about 1 hour), then continue by car to Arslanbob and Sary-Chelek. Overland travel from Bishkek is also possible via the Osh highway.

• Domestic flight Bishkek–Osh (about 1 hour)
• Private car transfer from Osh (included in tours)
• Overland via the Bishkek–Osh highway (10–12 hours)
