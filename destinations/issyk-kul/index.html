Issyk-Kul

The great lake, canyons and gorges of the Tien Shan

About Issyk-Kul

Issyk-Kul is one of the largest alpine lakes in the world, ringed by the Kungey and Terskey Ala-Too ranges. Around it are red-rock canyons, gorges, hot springs and the town of Karakol — a good add-on to a mountain trip or a relaxed stop on the way east.

Best timeApril–October
From Bishkek≈ 4–5 hours by car
Activities4x4, hiking, swimming
DifficultyEasy

Highlights

Skazka (Fairy Tale) Canyon
Red sandstone formations on the lake's southern shore.
Jety-Oguz
The 'Seven Bulls' red rock ridge near Karakol.
Karakol
The regional town, gateway to the Terskey Ala-Too for onward trekking.

Getting There

Issyk-Kul is reached via the Bishkek–Balykchy road along either the northern or southern shore, both scenic drives of half a day.

• Private car transfer from Bishkek (included in tours)
• Shared taxi or marshrutka from Bishkek's Vostok-5 station
• Northern shore (Cholpon-Ata) or southern shore (Bokonbaevo) routes
