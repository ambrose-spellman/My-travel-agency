Talas

The valley of Manas — quiet mountains and ancient history

About Talas

Talas is the least-visited corner of northern Kyrgyzstan, a fertile valley wedged between the Talas Ala-Too and Kyrgyz Ala-Too ranges. It's best known as the legendary homeland of Manas, the hero of Kyrgyzstan's national epic, and for its quiet, uncrowded mountains.

Best timeMay–September
From Bishkek5–6 hours by car
ActivitiesHiking, culture
DifficultyEasy to Moderate

Highlights

Manas Ordo
A memorial complex said to mark the burial site of Manas, with a small museum on the epic poem that forms the core of Kyrgyz national identity. A meaningful stop for understanding Kyrgyz culture.
Besh-Tash National Park
A forested valley in the Talas Ala-Too range, with glacial lakes and waterfalls reached by hiking trails. Far fewer visitors than the parks near Bishkek.

Getting There

Talas is reached via a scenic but long mountain road from Bishkek over the Too-Ashuu and Otmok passes, or via a longer route through Taraz in Kazakhstan.

• Shared taxi from Bishkek's western bus station (5–6 hours)
• Mountain road can close in heavy winter snow
• Best combined with a longer northern Kyrgyzstan itinerary
