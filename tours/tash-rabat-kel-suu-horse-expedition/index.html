Overview

A four-day horseback and 4x4 expedition from Naryn: ride over alpine passes to the 15th-century Tash-Rabat caravanserai, then continue by off-road vehicle into the Arpa and Aksai valleys to reach turquoise Kel-Suu lake, framed by sheer rock walls in the border zone near China.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Meet your guide in Naryn and transfer to the At-Bashy valley. A scenic ride through alpine pastures and high valleys. Dinner and overnight in a shepherd's yurt.

2

A full day on horseback over the pass to the 15th-century Tash-Rabat caravanserai. Explore the ancient halls before dinner and an overnight stay in a yurt camp nearby.

3

An off-road drive (about 6 hours) through the remote Arpa and Aksai valleys near the Chinese border. An evening walk, then dinner and overnight in a yurt camp near the lake.

4

Ride or hike to the turquoise lake at 3,500 m. Time for photos along the shore, then transfer back to Naryn.

Horse Riding & Safety

Kyrgyz horses are calm, sure-footed and used to mountain trails. Beginners are welcome: a horseman leads your horse on the first day and stays with the group on every ride.
