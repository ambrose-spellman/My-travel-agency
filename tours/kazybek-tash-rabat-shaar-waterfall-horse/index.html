Horseback Expedition: Kazybek-Tash-Rabat & Shaar Waterfall

Overview

Ride across the alpine passes of the At-Bashy range to the 15th-century Tash-Rabat caravanserai, then on to one of Central Asia's tallest waterfalls in the green Bash-Kaindy gorge — all in an open zone with no border permit needed.

✓Horseback ride into Bash-Kaindy gorge to Shaar Waterfall, one of Central Asia's tallest
✓No border permit needed — easy, last-minute-friendly booking

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Meet your guide in Naryn and transfer to Kazybek village in the At-Bashy valley. A scenic ride through alpine pastures and high valleys. Dinner and overnight in a yurt camp.

2

Excursion to the 15th-century caravanserai and a look through its ancient halls, with an overnight in a yurt camp nearby. A full day on horseback across mountain passes follows, with a viewpoint over Chatyr-Kul lake, before returning for dinner and a transfer to At-Bashy town for the night.

3

About 2.5 hours on horseback into the Bash-Kaindy gorge to Shaar Waterfall, for photos and a picnic, then about 2.5 hours back to the village. Overnight in a village yurt or homestay.

4

Transfer back to Naryn. Optionally, extend the day with a cultural programme in Naryn featuring an eagle-hunting show and nomad games such as Kok-boru and Kyz-Kuumai (extra cost, fixed per group, set by the eagle keeper).

Horse Riding & Safety

Kyrgyz horses are calm, sure-footed and used to mountain trails. Beginners are welcome: a horseman leads your horse on the first day and stays with the group on every ride.
