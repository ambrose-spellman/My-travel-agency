Naryn

Tash-Rabat – Arpa – Aksay – Kel-Suu 4x4

Duration 3–5 days Level Moderate Season June–September Group 2–6 people

Overview

Off-road loop via high mountain passes to the turquoise Kel-Suu lake with scenic stops along the way.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Early departure from Bishkek. Drive via Bishkek – Osh highway to Tash-Rabat caravanserai (approx. 8-9 hours). Explore the ancient Silk Road site. Overnight near Tash-Rabat.

2

Off-road adventure through Arpa valley and high mountain passes. Stunning views of Naryn region. Drive through Aksay canyon. Overnight in guesthouse or yurt camp.

3

Morning visit to Kel-Suu lake. Time for photos and short walks by the turquoise waters. Return journey to Bishkek (approx. 7-8 hours).
