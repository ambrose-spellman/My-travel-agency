Overview

Flexible road trip: Bishkek → Tokmok → Burana → Tash-Moinok → Issyk-Kul (Grigoryevka, Semenovka, Karakol, Jety-Oguz, Jomok-Say, Aksay, etc.). Fully customizable.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Depart Bishkek. Visit Tokmok city and Burana Tower (11th century minaret). Continue to Tash-Moinok canyon for spectacular views. Drive to Issyk-Kul lakeside (Grigoryevka or Semenovka). Overnight at lake.

2

Explore Issyk-Kul lake area. Options: Karakol city, Jety-Oguz rock formations, Grigoryevka gorge, Semenovka beach, or Jomok-Say canyon. Customizable based on preferences. Overnight at lake.

3

Morning at leisure by the lake. Return journey to Bishkek via scenic route. Optional stops en route. Tour ends in Bishkek in the evening.
