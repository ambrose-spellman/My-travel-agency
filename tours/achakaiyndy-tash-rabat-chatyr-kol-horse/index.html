Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Early morning departure from Bishkek. Drive to Achakaiyndy village (approx. 5-6 hours). Meet your horses and guide. Begin horse trek through scenic valleys. Overnight in yurt camp or guesthouse.

2

Full day of horse riding through dramatic At-Bashy gorges. Stunning mountain views, alpine meadows, and traditional nomadic settlements. Camping under the stars.

3

Continue through high mountain passes. Encounter local nomadic families. Learn about traditional Kyrgyz culture and hospitality. Overnight in yurt camp.

4

Ride to the historic Tash-Rabat caravanserai (15th century). Explore the ancient Silk Road trading post. Overnight near Tash-Rabat.

5

Depart Tash-Rabat and ride towards Chatyr-Kol lake. High altitude plateau with breathtaking views. Overnight by the lake.

6

Explore Chatyr-Kol lake and surrounding area. Optional short hikes. Relax and enjoy the pristine nature. Overnight in yurt camp.

7

Begin return journey through At-Bashy. Farewell dinner with local family. Overnight in guesthouse.

8

Final ride to Achakaiyndy. Drive back to Bishkek (5-6 hours). Tour ends in the evening.

Accommodation

Yurt camps, guesthouses, camping. Bedding and mats are provided; bring a warm sleeping bag for nights above 3,000 m.

Transport

Horses + 4x4 support. Starting point: Bishkek or Achakaiyndy. Ending point: Bishkek.

Horse Riding & Safety

Kyrgyz horses are calm, sure-footed and used to mountain trails. Beginners are welcome: a horseman leads your horse on the first day and stays with the group on every ride.
