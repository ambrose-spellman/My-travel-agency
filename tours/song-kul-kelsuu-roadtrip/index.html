Song-Kul – Kel-Suu Car Road Trip

Duration 3 days Level Easy Season June–September Group 2–6 people

Overview

A 3-day 4x4 road trip from the high pastures of Song-Kul lake onward to the remote turquoise waters of Kel-Suu — scenic mountain driving connecting two of Naryn's most striking lakes.

Highlights

✓Song-Kul alpine lake
✓Kel-Suu turquoise waters
✓Remote mountain driving routes
✓Jailoo pastures and yurts
✓Comfortable 4x4 travel
